# Analytics Measurement Plan

## Purpose

Turn business goals into events, dimensions, KPIs, and quality controls.

## When to Use

- A product needs analytics implementation or remediation.

## When Not to Use

- Goals, owners, or a lawful basis for collection are missing.

## Required Information

- Goals, journey, conversions, platforms, consent, and reporting needs.

## Procedure

1. Map business goals to decisions and measurable questions.
2. Define KPIs, events, properties, identities, and attribution rules.
3. Specify names, triggers, examples, and owners without ambiguity.
4. Apply data minimization, consent, and retention requirements.
5. Validate in development and production and monitor data quality.

## Validation

- Every finding or decision has evidence and an owner.
- Success criteria are checked before closing the task.
- Deviations are recorded with their next action.

## Expected Result

Versioned measurement plan with specifications and validation.

## Safety

Risk level: **medium**. Do not perform irreversible changes, production access, or credentialed operations without explicit authorization, a recoverable backup, and a rollback plan.
