# Conversion Rate Audit

## Purpose

Identify conversion friction and formulate measurable experiments.

## When to Use

- A journey has traffic and a defined conversion.

## When Not to Use

- There is no volume, reliable data, or ability to implement changes.

## Required Information

- Goal, funnel, segments, analytics, user research, and constraints.

## Procedure

1. Define conversion, segments, period, and guardrail metrics.
2. Verify data quality and locate drop-offs by stage.
3. Review value proposition, clarity, trust, forms, and errors.
4. Combine quantitative and qualitative evidence into concrete hypotheses.
5. Prioritize experiments by impact, confidence, effort, and risk.

## Validation

- Every finding or decision has evidence and an owner.
- Success criteria are checked before closing the task.
- Deviations are recorded with their next action.

## Expected Result

Backlog of prioritized hypotheses with metrics and test design.

## Safety

Risk level: **low**. Do not perform irreversible changes, production access, or credentialed operations without explicit authorization, a recoverable backup, and a rollback plan.
