# Technical SEO Audit

## Purpose

Find technical barriers affecting crawling, indexing, and organic performance.

## When to Use

- A site needs SEO diagnosis or is preparing a migration.

## When Not to Use

- Authorized access, goals, or target markets are unavailable.

## Required Information

- Domain, markets, CMS, analytics, Search Console, recent changes, and competitors.

## Procedure

1. Define scope, markets, templates, and organic goals.
2. Crawl within approved limits and compare results with search engine data.
3. Review indexing, canonicals, robots, sitemaps, links, and HTTP statuses.
4. Assess rendering, structured data, performance, and duplicate content.
5. Prioritize actions and define how to measure their effect after implementation.

## Validation

- Every finding or decision has evidence and an owner.
- Success criteria are checked before closing the task.
- Deviations are recorded with their next action.

## Expected Result

Prioritized report with evidence, impact, and validation criteria.

## Safety

Risk level: **low**. Do not perform irreversible changes, production access, or credentialed operations without explicit authorization, a recoverable backup, and a rollback plan.
