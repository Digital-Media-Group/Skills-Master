# Project Discovery

## Purpose

Align the problem, outcomes, scope, risks, and validation plan before execution.

## When to Use

- An initiative must turn an idea into verifiable decisions.

## When Not to Use

- The goal is to confirm a solution without understanding the problem or users.

## Required Information

- Business context, users, evidence, constraints, owners, and timeline.

## Procedure

1. Identify sponsors, users, affected parties, and decision owners.
2. State the problem, current evidence, and expected outcomes.
3. Separate needs, assumptions, constraints, and proposed solutions.
4. Prioritize risks and design the cheapest validation for each one.
5. Align scope, exclusions, metrics, and next decisions.

## Validation

- Every finding or decision has evidence and an owner.
- Success criteria are checked before closing the task.
- Deviations are recorded with their next action.

## Expected Result

Validated brief, risk map, initial scope, and next decisions.

## Safety

Risk level: **low**. Do not perform irreversible changes, production access, or credentialed operations without explicit authorization, a recoverable backup, and a rollback plan.
