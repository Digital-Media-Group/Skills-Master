# Software Estimation

## Purpose

Produce an explainable estimate with a range, assumptions, risks, and confidence level.

## When to Use

- There is enough scope to plan or compare alternatives.

## When Not to Use

- An exact date is demanded without evidence or uncertainty allowance.

## Required Information

- Scope, acceptance criteria, architecture, team, dependencies, and constraints.

## Procedure

1. Clarify the decision the estimate must support.
2. Break scope into verifiable deliverables and dependencies.
3. Identify unknown work, integrations, quality, and operations.
4. Estimate with ranges using documented historical or comparable data.
5. Include contingency, confidence, assumptions, and a review date.

## Validation

- Every finding or decision has evidence and an owner.
- Success criteria are checked before closing the task.
- Deviations are recorded with their next action.

## Expected Result

Effort or schedule range with explicit assumptions and risks.

## Safety

Risk level: **low**. Do not perform irreversible changes, production access, or credentialed operations without explicit authorization, a recoverable backup, and a rollback plan.
