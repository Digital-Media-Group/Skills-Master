# Cloud Architecture Review

## Purpose

Assess cloud architecture for reliability, security, performance, and cost.

## When to Use

- A solution diagram or inventory exists.

## When Not to Use

- Business, data, and compliance boundaries are undefined.

## Required Information

- Architecture, workloads, SLOs, data, threats, costs, and regulatory constraints.

## Procedure

1. Confirm business goals, SLOs, and responsibility boundaries.
2. Inventory components, data flows, identities, and dependencies.
3. Assess resilience, security, operations, performance, and cost.
4. Test failure and recovery scenarios using available evidence.
5. Prioritize changes by risk, value, effort, and dependencies.

## Validation

- Every finding or decision has evidence and an owner.
- Success criteria are checked before closing the task.
- Deviations are recorded with their next action.

## Expected Result

Risk assessment and prioritized improvement roadmap.

## Safety

Risk level: **high**. Do not perform irreversible changes, production access, or credentialed operations without explicit authorization, a recoverable backup, and a rollback plan.
