# Web Performance Audit

## Purpose

Identify bottlenecks and propose measurable web performance improvements.

## When to Use

- A site is slow or needs a performance baseline.

## When Not to Use

- Permission to measure the target environment is unavailable.

## Required Information

- URLs, devices, markets, available analytics, and performance goals.

## Procedure

1. Define pages, devices, locations, and test conditions.
2. Capture lab metrics and field data when available.
3. Analyze network, rendering, JavaScript, images, fonts, and third parties.
4. Link each issue to evidence and user impact.
5. Prioritize by impact, effort, and risk; repeat measurements after changes.

## Validation

- Every finding or decision has evidence and an owner.
- Success criteria are checked before closing the task.
- Deviations are recorded with their next action.

## Expected Result

Baseline, verified causes, and a prioritized plan with target metrics.

## Safety

Risk level: **low**. Do not perform irreversible changes, production access, or credentialed operations without explicit authorization, a recoverable backup, and a rollback plan.
