# Web Project Bootstrap

## Purpose

Prepare a maintainable web foundation with initial decisions, structure, and controls.

## When to Use

- A new project needs a repeatable technical foundation.

## When Not to Use

- Scope and technology have not yet been agreed.

## Required Information

- Goals, users, constraints, approved stack, and delivery criteria.

## Procedure

1. Confirm scope, owners, and constraints before selecting tools.
2. Inspect existing standards and reuse them when applicable.
3. Create the minimum structure, environment configuration, and development commands.
4. Add formatting, testing, build, and onboarding controls.
5. Record relevant decisions and validate the flow from a clean clone.

## Validation

- Every finding or decision has evidence and an owner.
- Success criteria are checked before closing the task.
- Deviations are recorded with their next action.

## Expected Result

Initial repository, conventions, verifiable commands, and decision record.

## Safety

Risk level: **low**. Do not perform irreversible changes, production access, or credentialed operations without explicit authorization, a recoverable backup, and a rollback plan.
